The plan

TMC in the morning. UDS from the afternoon. Food, entertainment, old stories and time to catch up.

Important: Those coming only to TMC need to pay a nominal amount of ₹ 1500. All classmates visiting or staying at UDS are asked to contribute the additional ₹5,000 per person towards the common GTG expenses listed below.

How to pay

A few members of the organizing committee will contact the classmates allocated to them. The ₹5,000 common contribution can be paid directly to the contacting committee member.
